To you,

My dearest,

If I could put everything I feel into words, I would probably run out of paper long before I ran out of things to say. So I made this little place for you instead — three thousand little “I love you”s, gathered together to make one heart.

I love you in the quiet moments, when nothing needs to be said. I love you in the silly moments that make absolutely no sense to anyone else. I love the memories we already have, and I love the thought of all the little moments that are still waiting for us.

There are so many beautiful things in this world, but somehow my heart keeps choosing the thought of you. You have a way of making an ordinary day feel a little warmer, a little softer, a little more worth remembering.

I don't want this letter to promise some perfect story. I just want it to say something simple and honest: you matter to me. More than these three thousand words could ever explain.

So whenever you doubt how special you are, come back here. Look at this heart and remember that every tiny “I love you” inside it is carrying the same message: I'm grateful that you exist.

And if I had to write it another three thousand times, I would. Then another three thousand after that.

With all my heart,
